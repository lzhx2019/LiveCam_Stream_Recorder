## English

**LiveCam Stream Recorder — Privacy Policy**

Effective date: 2026-11-07

**Summary: This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

**What the extension does.** LiveCam Stream Recorder records the video stream of the page you are currently viewing, entirely inside your browser, and saves the result to your own device as an MP4 or WebM file. Recording starts only after you click the extension icon and press Start in the panel.

**What data is collected.** None. The extension has no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no network requests to any server. All recording and file assembly happens locally in your browser.

**What stays on your device.** The recorded media file, saved wherever your browser downloads files. The extension does not read, index, or upload it.

**Permissions and why they are needed.**

- `activeTab` — grants temporary access to the current tab only when you click the extension toolbar icon.
- `scripting` — used to inject the panel script into the current tab at that moment.

The extension holds no host permissions of any kind: pages you have not clicked the icon on are never touched, and nothing runs in the background.

**Contact.** For questions about this policy, contact the developer listed on the Chrome Web Store listing page.

## 中文

**LiveCam Stream Recorder — 隐私政策**

生效日期:2026-11-07

**摘要:本扩展不收集、不存储、不传输任何个人数据。**

**扩展做什么。** LiveCam Stream Recorder 在你的浏览器内录制你当前浏览页面的视频流,并把成果保存到你自己的设备上,格式为 MP4 或 WebM。只有当你点击扩展图标并在面板中按下「开始」后,录制才会发生。

**收集哪些数据。** 没有。本扩展没有分析统计、没有遥测上报、没有崩溃监测,也不会向任何服务器发出网络请求。录制与文件封装全部在浏览器本地完成。

**哪些数据留在本机。** 录制得到的媒体文件,保存在你浏览器的下载目录里。扩展不读取、不索引、不上传它。

**权限及其用途。**

- `activeTab`——仅当你点击扩展工具栏图标时,临时授予对当前标签页的访问。
- `scripting`——在该时刻向当前标签页注入面板脚本。

本扩展不持有任何主机权限:凡是你未点击图标的页面,扩展完全不接触,后台也不运行任何代码。

**联系方式。** 有关本政策的疑问,请通过 Chrome Web Store 商店里列出的开发者联系方式反馈。
